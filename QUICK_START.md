# Pressure Room — Quick Start

Pressure Room is a screenwriting workspace that tracks **what a character wants, what pressure makes them choose, and what every choice costs**.

## The core loop

**Want → Pressure → Choice → Consequence → Bill → Change**

A scene should not exist simply because “something happens.” Ideally the scene changes what is possible next.

## Your first ten minutes

1. **Create a character.** Give them a Want and a Moral Boundary: the thing they believe they will not do.
2. **Write the scene first.** Use **Write** mode and get words on the page.
3. **Open Structure only when useful.** Record the scene’s Want, Pressure, and Choice. The deeper fields are optional.
4. **Connect scenes with THEREFORE / BUT.** Ask why the next scene must happen because of the previous one.
5. **Record the bill.** External bill = what the choice caused in the world. Moral cost = what making the choice did to the character.
6. **Diagnose after writing.** Story MRI and Room Questions are lenses, not grades.

## The four areas

### Write
The screenplay is the main surface. Scene structure stays hidden until you open it.

### Structure
Story spine, characters, causality, bills, branches, and snapshots. One structural task at a time.

### Diagnose
Story MRI, Pressure Lab, and optional Writers’ Room questions.

### How to use
The in-app version of this guide for new writers and collaborators.

## Sharing

Use **Share** to export:

- `.pressureroom` — the complete editable project
- PDF — a human-readable story packet
- Markdown — portable story notes
- Fountain — screenplay text for Fountain-compatible software

A collaborator can import a `.pressureroom` package **as a copy** or use **restore/replace** when intentionally restoring the canonical version.
